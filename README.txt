KOTA FRESH VEGGIE - clickable customer-app prototype
Open index.html in Chrome/Edge on a phone or computer.

Included:
- Premium white/green Modern Fresh UI
- Home, categories/products, search
- Add to cart and quantity controls
- Checkout with Online Payment / Cash on Delivery selection
- Delivery address
- Order placement and tracking demo
- Profile page

This is a UI/functional prototype, not yet a production APK.
Production APK needs a backend/database, real payment gateway, authentication,
delivery/order management and Android build/signing configuration.
